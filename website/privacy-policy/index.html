2. Zero Third-Party Trackers on the Web Portal

The ODCS Web Portal is engineered with a Pure Sovereign Architecture. Unlike conventional single-page applications that bundle megabytes of surveillance trackers, our infrastructure enforces the following strict invariants:

✓ Zero Tracking Cookies

We do not place tracking, advertising, or profiling cookies on your browser.

✓ Zero Google Analytics or Pixels

No external telemetry scripts report your browsing behavior or IP address to tech conglomerates.

✓ Zero npm & React Bloat

Constructed with native HTML5, CSS3, and app.wasm (4.9 KB) executed sandboxed in WebAssembly.

✓ Local Client Storage

Simulation states and Merkle tree logs remain strictly inside your browser's memory and IndexedDB.

3. ODCS Assistant Telegram Bot (@odcs_assistant_bot) Data Governance

The ODCS Assistant is a 24/7 background systemd daemon running on our sovereign Linux VPS (103.71.99.204). To provide technical assistance, answer developer questions, and deliver release binaries, the bot processes minimal technical information per the Principle of Data Minimization:

A. Information We Process

  • Numerical Telegram User ID: A unique numerical ID assigned by Telegram, strictly required to deliver answers, enforce rate-limiting (20 requests/minute to prevent Denial-of-Service), and maintain your broadcast notification preferences.
  • Username & First Name: Processed temporarily to personalize chat responses (e.g. "Welcome, Alex!") and identify voluntary feedback submissions.
  • Voluntary Feedback: Text messages explicitly submitted by you via the /feedback command.
  • Anonymized Query Telemetry: Query keywords submitted to /ask are evaluated in memory against local markdown specifications to calculate documentation relevance scores.

B. Information We NEVER Collect or Store

  • We never access, collect, or store your Telegram account password, phone number, or session authorization tokens.
  • We never sell, license, rent, or trade user identifiers or interaction histories to advertising brokers or marketing databases.
  • We never execute arbitrary shell commands or unsanitized code submitted through Telegram.

C. Channel Announcements & Instant Opt-Out

The bot provides infrequent technical broadcasts regarding major standard ratifications and security advisories. You have absolute control over your notification preferences:

• To opt out of all broadcasts immediately, send: /optout
• To re-enable release notifications at any time, send: /optin
• To verify your Telegram identity and admin tier, send: /id

4. The ODCS Privacy-by-Design Invariant (Layer 4 Zero Intent Loss)

Under the Open Digital Continuity Standard (ODCS-2.0.0 GA), software applications are designed around Local Hardware Authority. In traditional cloud architectures, your typed forms and sensitive inputs are streamed in plaintext across remote servers before you receive confirmation.

Under ODCS Layer 4:

🛡️ Sub-16ms Cryptographic Receipt Sealing: Every intent is committed to local NVMe/IndexedDB write-ahead logs (WAL) and sealed with an RFC 8785 canonical SHA-256 Merkle hash before any network transmission is attempted. You retain full cryptographic ownership of your state.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Security Architecture

Telegram bot telemetry and voluntary feedback are stored in a dedicated SQLite database operating in Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) mode. Access is restricted to the host filesystem with strict Unix permissions (chmod 600 .env).

Data retention timelines:

  • User Profiles: Retained while active; deleted upon receiving an opt-out or purge request.
  • Feedback Submissions: Stored locally for engineering review and pruned periodically.
  • Query Logs: Anonymized and aggregated for system diagnostics; automatically cycled.

6. Your Data Rights & 48-Hour Permanent Purge Guarantee

In full accordance with international data protection principles (including the EU GDPR and California CCPA), you have the right to inspect, correct, export, or permanently delete all records associated with your Telegram account:

  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request complete erasure of your User ID, feedback, and telemetry from our database.
  • SLA Commitment: All deletion requests are executed by our standards leads within 48 hours.
How to submit a Data Deletion Request:
Send a direct request on Telegram to our maintainers at t.me/opendigitalcontinuity or email standards@odcs.in specifying your Telegram User ID.
